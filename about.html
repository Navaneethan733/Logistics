Our Story

Built from a Single Shipping Desk

Stackly Logistics was founded in 2009 by a team of freight professionals who believed that logistics could be smarter, more transparent, and genuinely client-focused. Starting with a single trucking route between Salem and Chennai, we grew organically by putting customers first — always.

Today, we operate a fully integrated multimodal logistics network with dedicated air freight desks, ocean cargo partnerships with 40+ shipping lines, and bonded warehouse facilities across 12 Indian cities.

Our technology-first culture means clients get real-time visibility, predictive analytics, and exception management — not just a shipment tracking number.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

15 years of growth, innovation, and expanding our global footprint.

2009

Founded in Salem

Stackly Logistics launched with 5 employees and one trucking contract between Salem and Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

2012

First Ocean Freight Desk

Expanded into sea freight with FCL services from Chennai and Tuticorin ports, covering 15 international routes.

2015

IATA Accreditation & Air Cargo Launch

Received IATA accreditation and launched our air cargo division with partnerships with 6 major airlines.

2018

TMS & WMS Platform Launch

Released our proprietary Transport Management System and Warehouse Management System, enabling real-time client tracking.

2021

120+ Countries Network

Established agent partnerships in 120+ countries, enabling door-to-door freight globally from India.

2024

AI-Powered Logistics Intelligence

Integrated AI-driven route optimization and predictive analytics, reducing average freight cost for clients by 18%.
